Forge Warden's Handbook

The star burns at the conduit's end. Void constructs ride the molten path inward to drink its light. Every leak tears starlight out — at 0%, the star goes dark.

Towers are forged along the path: Bolt (fast shots), Frost (slows), Mortar (splash), Tesla (chain lightning). Upgrade twice, then pick a specialization.

Stage rewards: after each stage, the forge refines what the star sheds — pick 1 of 3 gifts.

STARFALL: kills gather flare. At full charge, press F or the STARFALL button, then click the conduit — the star itself answers, hurling a fragment of its own fire.

Calling a stage early pays bonus shards. Selling a tower refunds 70%.
